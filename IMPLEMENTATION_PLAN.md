# Honeymoon Atlas implementation plan

**Goal.** Open the planner in Bangkok, compare a few worthwhile next stops, and freely search Google Maps for another. Pick a beach resort, restaurants and activities for a stay, see how the decision changes the whole honeymoon, then confirm it. The map, right panel, dates and budget should always describe the same selection.

**Trip defaults.** Two people, 10 January through 31 March 2027, 80 nights, Bangkok arrival and Seoul departure. Follow Thailand → Vietnam → Japan → South Korea. Aim for 40 beach nights, a continuous seven-night honeymoon stay, 8–10 stops, up to eight nights in Tokyo and a short Seoul finish (three nights by default). The €15,000 budget is for the couple. Keep these preferences editable and show whether the booked international flights are included in the budget. Preserve the original candidate routes for comparison.

## What a finished decision feels like

From Bangkok, select a suggested branch or search for **Ko Lanta**, which is absent from the baseline. A city, island or dropped pin can be an overnight base. A restaurant or spa can be attached to a stay without consuming a night. Switch the panel between Stay, Hotels, Eat, Do, Shops and Scores. Clicking a map result or its matching panel entry selects the same place. A hotel can remain undecided while planning.

Compare two branches against the same confirmed route. Each shows the arrival and departure dates, beach nights gained, continuous resort nights, transfer burden, total known cost for two, outstanding costs, and the top reason its rank changed. A change in the preview never advances confirmed progress. On **Add stop**, the line becomes solid, the route and budget update together, and the panel offers the next decision. **Undo** restores the exact previous route. A partner can open a named copy via export/import and compare it with the original.

## Contracts all subagents share

- **Trip and draft.** Use a versioned trip with a revision, ordered stays, preferences, locks and budget assumptions. A stay has its own stable visit ID, a destination reference, local check-in date, nights, optional lodging choice and chosen venue references. Two Hanoi visits may refer to one Google place and have different hotels, notes and dates. Date arithmetic uses local calendar nights across time zones and midnight flights. Store transfer/overnight travel explicitly when it occupies a night.
- **Places and money.** A reference can point to a catalog entry, a Google Place ID, or a user-defined pin. Map discovery, verified user quotes and workbook estimates carry distinct provenance. Money records include currency, party size, rate basis, applicable dates, source and observation date. Preserve original amounts and the conversion basis when showing euros. Separate paid/booked, estimated, and unknown components. An unknown hotel price never produces a €0 stay or improves its affordability rank.
- **Interaction.** A single selection carries visit ID, place reference, role and draft revision. Atomic edit commands drive map, panel, summary and persistence. An async result can update only its matching query, category, area and revision. Keep the viewport and panel width out of route history. Saving a named version or undoing a change must be safe after a reload or import.
- **Google as the live source.** Use Maps JavaScript for the map, Places for discovery, and Routes for supported ground/transit legs. The existing catalog and personal inputs supply offline planning and evidence that Maps does not publish. A Maps price tier is not a dated room quote. Flights, bookable rooms, ferry seat inventory, internet quality, cleanliness, safety and blossom dates often need separate evidence. Show an explicit unknown, a dated independent estimate or a user quote. Flight booking and ticketing are outside this release.

### Google discovery and routing

1. **Configure the surface first.** Verify the billing account's actual region, enabled products, browser referrers and API restrictions without printing any credential. Use Google’s Places UI Kit for provider-rendered venue content beside the map. For a project subject to the EEA terms, the adjacent panel counts as use with a map. Ordinary Places data has specific restrictions; Google documents a UI Kit exception. Use permitted identifiers and positions for pin synchronization and keep Google and third-party attribution visible at every panel size. For EEA Routes, use permitted geometry, distance and duration in the map view and omit provider route descriptions and turn-by-turn steps there. [Places guidance](https://developers.google.com/maps/comms/eea/places) · [Routes guidance](https://developers.google.com/maps/comms/eea/routes)
2. **Separate the credential roles.** Load a browser key restricted to the two localhost origins at port 8080. The UI Kit needs its service enabled and an appropriate referrer-restricted browser configuration. If the existing key is restricted to Maps JavaScript alone, surface the missing capability and obtain approval for the exact restriction change or supported separate setup. Server-side Places and Routes calls each use their own API-limited key. Keep server credentials out of static JS, runtime browser configuration, images, exports, logs and Git. Ship a blank example and ignore local secrets. Bind the server through the local Compose service; accept only specific operations and validated request parameters. No billing changes or live calls in automated CI. [UI Kit setup](https://developers.google.com/maps/documentation/javascript/places-ui-kit/get-started) · [Key restrictions](https://developers.google.com/maps/api-security-best-practices)
3. **Search the active area on demand.** Start with destination autocomplete, native POI clicks and map pins. Offer lodging, beaches, hiking, boats, tea, spa/beauty, massage, vegetarian/traditional/unusual dining, Muay Thai, culture, history, nightlife and shopping as query presets. Support custom text, town/radius or visible area, and an explicit **Search this area** action. Use only the fields needed, fetch details on selection and offer further results where supported. Respect provider ranking and bounds. Nearby Search returns up to 20; REST Text Search currently returns up to 60 across pages. Do not crawl surrounding tiles to manufacture a complete directory. [Nearby Search](https://developers.google.com/maps/documentation/places/web-service/nearby-search) · [Text Search](https://developers.google.com/maps/documentation/places/web-service/text-search)
4. **Join by identity.** A selectable map POI and a UI Kit selection both dispatch the same place ID. An unlabeled map click can become a user pin. Google renders its venue details; keep the couple’s notes, choices, quotes and criteria in app-owned controls. The UI Kit exposes IDs, location and viewport. Do not extract widget text or mine reviews for personalized scores. Store eligible identifiers and user-authored data; rehydrate Google details on demand. Keep raw provider responses, photos, reviews and polylines out of durable saves, backups and exports by default. Follow applicable attribution, privacy and retention requirements. [UI Kit migration and event model](https://developers.google.com/maps/documentation/javascript/adopt-places-ui-kit) · [Places policies](https://developers.google.com/maps/documentation/places/web-service/policies)
5. **Request transport selectively.** Obtain supported travel times for shortlisted legs, then get the selected leg's geometry. Count matrix elements when budgeting requests. Label incomplete ferry/transit links and air travel separately; let the couple enter a confirmed schedule or fare. A timetable outside Google's supported transit horizon stays unresolved. Make panning, resizing, animating or reopening a panel generate zero new searches unless the user initiates one. Use request deduplication, per-operation limits, a local allowance and a per-session usage view. Google Cloud budget alerts notify; enforce supported quotas and local request ceilings separately. [Transit limits](https://developers.google.com/maps/documentation/routes/transit-route) · [Cost controls](https://developers.google.com/maps/billing-and-pricing/manage-costs)

### Suggestions and budget decisions

Filter hard violations before ranking. Enforce locked flights, country order, required destinations and date ranges; respect the configured Tokyo cap and any other hard limits the couple sets. Keep budget status separate from schedule feasibility. A known minimum over €15,000 is over budget; an estimate that straddles the budget is at risk; substantial unknowns make affordability unresolved. Editing a past stay previews affected downstream dates and the Seoul flight. Never shorten a locked stay to conceal a conflict.

Begin with the current 35/65 weighting as a comparison baseline. Generate next-stop proposals from nearby candidates, onward hubs and both partners’ saved places so an island or a Japan gateway can compete with a geographically closer city. Use a bounded beam search with a documented expansion limit and stable tie-breaks. On that frontier, retain the candidates that trade off cost, beach nights, honeymoon lodging, variety and time spent moving. Show up to three materially different feasible options, for example **lowest known cost**, **more beach time**, and **fewer transfers**. If fewer alternatives have evidence, display fewer. Show an estimated continuation through Seoul and identify its assumptions. A user-selected stop can still be explored when no fully verified continuation exists. On tiny synthetic graphs compare the optimizer to exhaustive enumeration. On larger graphs keep a deterministic cap and report the best route found within the search.

For each original criterion — price, cleanliness, safety, accessibility, internet, authenticity — show the measurement, source, date, confidence and weight. Pre-fill the shared honeymoon profile from existing preferences. Either partner can adjust weights; an optional side-by-side view shows when changing a weight flips a recommendation. Planning should work before either person fills in a questionnaire. Retain user-entered judgments and independently sourced catalog evidence. Show the weighted score across observed criteria **and** evidence coverage. A pair of well-rated fields cannot silently outrank a well-evidenced stay on six criteria. Apply diminishing returns for repeats; reward contiguous beach/resort nights and penalize packing/transfers. Track forecast windows and event dates independently of discovering a venue on Maps.

## Release checks

1. **Real discovery.** With owner-authorized credentials and a small request allowance, find a base absent from the bundled 16, open a hotel from its map pin, add a vegetarian restaurant and a beach, select a different hotel, and inspect the budget delta. Compare, cancel, commit and undo. Record pass/fail and request counts without recording keys or raw provider payloads.
2. **Trip mathematics.** Allocate exactly 80 local nights and end in Seoul on 31 March. Test R072 with the default Tokyo cap, an unpriced flight, a paid excursion and a meal covered by the daily allowance. Verify that prices for two, locked dates, known minimums and missing evidence survive every edit. Both editors must give the same completion and budget status.
3. **Persistence.** Import both legacy formats, load a named version, save a dynamic base and two distinct visits to one city, restart Docker, reload and export/import into a fresh browser profile. Compare IDs, dates, selections, money and locks. Unknown or obsolete place IDs surface a recoverable reselect action.
4. **Interaction.** At 1440, 1024, 390 and 320 CSS pixels, test resize during search, orientation changes, 200% zoom, keyboard-only selection and reduced motion. No clipped confirm button, stolen focus, unrequested search, moving camera or lost scroll position. Verify the active panel after rapid A → B selection when A's response arrives last.
5. **Operational fallback.** Exercise a missing or misrestricted key, empty search, quota exhaustion, provider timeout, a date outside transit coverage and loss of network midway through an edit. Static data stays available with its snapshot label. Credentials and restricted provider content never appear in logs, browser bundles, exports, Git or Docker layers. Build on a clean clone using the documented Compose command.

**First implementation PR.** Check in the two legacy fixtures, the R072 and duplicate-Hanoi regression tests, and a versioned trip-state adapter that accepts one uncatalogued base with unknown accommodation. Then build the Ko Lanta flow against the same contract in parallel. This makes the first Google-backed decision usable before the rest of the interface is redesigned.
